    QRadar Offense Intelligence¶

    AI-powered SOC investigation platform for IBM QRadar. On-device LLM analysis · MITRE ATT&CK mapping · Campaign detection · Zero data egress.

    Offense Detail — AI analysis verdict, MITRE kill chain, and structured investigation workspace.

    What is it?¶

    QRadar Offense Intelligence is a native IBM QRadar application that transforms how SOC teams investigate, manage, and respond to security offenses.

    QRadar surfaces offenses with raw metadata — rule names, source IPs, event counts. Turning that into a confident verdict requires an analyst to manually query events, look up rule logic, cross-reference historical patterns, and consult runbooks. At scale, this takes 30 minutes to several hours per offense and becomes the primary bottleneck in Mean Time to Respond (MTTR).

    QRadar Offense Intelligence eliminates that bottleneck. It embeds the Qwen3.5-9B large language model, running locally via llama.cpp directly on the QRadar appliance, and delivers a structured AI threat analysis — verdict, kill chain, recommended actions — in under two minutes. No security data ever leaves the customer's environment.

    Core Capabilities¶

    • On-Device AI Analysis

      The LLM runs entirely on your QRadar appliance. Structured 7-section threat analysis per offense — verdict, attack narrative, MITRE mapping, affected assets, recommended actions, false positive assessment, and executive summary.

    • MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

      Automatic technique tagging via on-device LLM, validated against a local ATT&CK knowledge base. Analyst overrides and team knowledge base promotion. Full offline support.

    • Attack Campaign Detection

      Automatically clusters related offenses into named attack campaigns. Each campaign shows a kill chain progression, date range, severity, analyst assignments, and lateral movement warnings.

    • Relationship Graph

      Interactive network graph with 5 investigative lenses: Rule Topology, Attacker Topology, Target Topology, Campaign Topology, and Lateral Movement chain.

    • Investigation Workspace

      Collaborative notes, evidence file attachments, IOC artifact tracking, response task lists, and SOP playbooks — all bound to the offense inside QRadar.

    • SLA Management & Analytics

      Real-time MTTD, MTTR, MTTA tracking, SLA breach notifications, analyst workload distribution, and stalled case identification.

    Key Differentiator¶

    Zero Data Egress

    The AI model (Qwen3.5-9B Q5) runs on the QRadar appliance itself. No offense data, event payloads, IP addresses, or usernames are sent to any external service — ever.

    This makes QRadar Offense Intelligence deployable in regulated, classified, and air-gapped environments where cloud AI is not permitted.
